MY APPROACH

Curiosity first.
Progress always.

I like taking an idea and working out how to turn it into something people can actually use. Sometimes that means designing a website, sometimes building a game, and sometimes figuring out how different pieces of a larger application fit together.

Along the way, I experiment with frontend interfaces, backend services, databases, and AI-powered features. I care about making projects useful, easy to understand, and better with every iteration.

I'm still learning, and that's part of the fun. Each bug, feature, and new project teaches me something I can bring to the next build.
